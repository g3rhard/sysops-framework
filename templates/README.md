# SysOps Framework — Template Pack

Copy, adapt, break, and rebuild these for your team.

## Contents

| File                              | Primary guidance | What It's For                                |
| --------------------------------- | ---------------- | -------------------------------------------- |
| `work-intake-triage.md`           | Chapter 3        | Classify and route incoming work             |
| `baseline-metrics.md`             | Chapter 7        | Establish an honest pilot baseline           |
| `pilot-retrospective.md`          | Getting Started  | Make the pilot Go / Adapt / Stop decision    |
| `capability-radar.csv`            | Chapter 8        | Collect team capability scores               |
| `service-inventory.md`            | Chapter 6        | Record services, owners, dependencies, SLOs  |
| `severity-matrix.md`              | Chapter 6        | Apply one SEV1–SEV4 incident scale           |
| `post-incident-review.md`         | Chapter 6        | Blameless post-incident review / post-mortem |
| `incident-commander-checklist.md` | Chapter 6        | Pocket card for the Incident Commander role  |
| `change-control-form.md`          | Chapter 6        | Change request form with rollback plan       |
| `sla-template.md`                 | Chapter 6        | Service Level Agreement skeleton             |
| `on-call-policy-template.md`      | Chapter 9        | On-call rotation policy blueprint            |
| `shift-handoff.md`                | Chapter 9        | Transfer active risk between responders      |
| `risk-register.md`                | Chapter 10       | Track risk ownership and treatment decisions |
| `reporting-template.md`           | Chapter 7        | Monthly one-page ops report for stakeholders |

## Usage

Copy the files you need, remove fields that don't apply, add your organization's specifics, and store them in version control alongside your operational documentation.
